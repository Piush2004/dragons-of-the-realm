2. Sound design.
3. Collision with castles, walls and towers.
4. Ground-fight polish: grapple, tail whip, wing-shove, better walk and folded wings.

6. Texture and geometry detail on the dragons (displaced scales, per-scale normal detail, skin wrinkles).
