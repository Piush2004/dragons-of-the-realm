# Emberbond

A realistic, third-person dragon story game in Unity 6.3 (HDRP). You are born in a dragon-riding house, bond with a
wyrm, and ride it through a succession war. See `Design/` for the story and design documents and the two reference
images the look is aimed at.

## What is playable now (vertical slice)

* Hearthmoor: a walled port city on a hill with a keep, gate, harbour, market and ruins, on a procedurally generated
  volcanic island with forests, rock, sand and sea.
* A throne-room council with three timed, tone-tagged choices ([Diplomatic] [Aggressive] [Deceptive]) that change
  Honour, Fear and Cunning.
* Street fight: sword strikes with aim assist, a timed parry that staggers attackers, stamina, soldiers that wind up
  visible blows.
* Summon and mount your wyrm; chase-camera flight with boost and dive, fireballs with real lights, a rival wyrm
  boss with a health bar; quest banner, compass with objective, sigil health badge.
* A procedurally animated, rigged rider and dragon (wing beats, tail, jaw), generated in Blender.

## Controls

Keyboard and mouse or gamepad. WASD move, Shift sprint, Space jump, LMB strike / breathe fire, RMB parry,
E mount or land, G summon mount, 1-3 dialogue choices.

## Building

Everything is generated by scripts; nothing is hand-authored in the editor.

```bash
# 1. Art (Blender 5.2, headless)
blender --background --python tools/blender/make_terrain_textures.py -- Assets/Art/Terrain 2048
blender --background --python tools/blender/make_vegetation.py  -- <out>   # copy into Assets/Art/Vegetation
blender --background --python tools/blender/make_city.py        -- <out>   # copy into Assets/Art/City
blender --background --python tools/blender/make_characters.py  -- <out>   # copy rider.fbx into Assets/Art/Characters
blender --background --python tools/blender/make_hall.py        -- <out>   # copy into Assets/Art/Hall

# 2. Scene and Windows player (Unity 6000.3.26f1)
Unity.exe -batchmode -nographics -quit -projectPath Emberbond -executeMethod Emberbond.EditorTools.IslandBuilder.Build
Unity.exe -batchmode -nographics -quit -projectPath Emberbond -executeMethod Emberbond.EditorTools.IslandBuilder.BuildPlayer
```

Run `Builds/Windows/Emberbond.exe`. Useful flags: `-autoplay` (scripted session with screenshots into `play/`),
`-quality high|low`, `-nohall`, `-nosoldiers`, `-nocity`.

## Graphics tiers

On a discrete GPU everything is on. On integrated graphics the game switches off volumetric clouds and fog, screen
space reflections, shortens shadows and tree distance (see `QualityGovernor.cs`). Measured on a laptop with an RTX 5060
the slice ran at 60-100+ fps at 1080p; on the same laptop's Radeon 780M it runs at about 15 fps.

## Known limits

* Art is procedural: houses, towers, characters and dragons are stylised low-poly with PBR materials, not
  hand-sculpted. Faces are not modelled.
* Only chapters 1-2 of the ten in the design are playable; the Pit descent, the mounted charge, the aerial duel in
  full, the Pale Tide act and the three endings are designed (`Design/story_v2_reference_driven.md`) but not built.
* Entering the throne hall for the first time stalls for several seconds while its shaders compile.
* No save system, menus, settings, audio or localisation yet.
